Ethics Week Returns Superadmin Thu, 10/15/2026 - 14:28 Ethics Week returns this year with a focus on “ Ethics in Action ,” highlighting how Georgia Tech’s core value — We act ethically — comes to life in our campus community. Ethics is not just a principle; it is the everyday decisions, conversations, and commitments that build trust and integrity into everything we do. “Ethics in Action is about recognizing that integrity isn’t something we save for the big moments,” said Joshua Toas, chief ethics and compliance officer.
